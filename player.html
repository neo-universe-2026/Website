    How pictures become sound

    The jukebox reads each frame of a video and turns what it sees into music.

    Colour becomes harmony
    Warm colours lean towards bright, major chords. Cool colours lean towards darker, minor ones.
    Brightness becomes pitch
    Bright areas play higher notes. Dark areas play lower notes.
    Busy areas become rhythm
    Crowded, detailed parts of the picture play faster rhythms. Calm, empty parts slow down.
    Change over time becomes a story
    As the video moves on, the music changes with it, so you hear the planet change.
