    Sonification engine

    How pictures become sound

    The Sonificator engine samples each video frame on a coarse grid and writes the soundtrack on the fly. Here is the mapping it uses.

    Colour becomes energy
    Strongly coloured pixels of any hue (black, white and grey are ignored) raise the energy: louder layers, a faster tempo between 68 and 96 beats per minute, and a denser groove, from Sparse Drift up to Intense Climax.
    Position becomes space and register
    The centre of the colour pans the music left or right, and colour low in the picture lifts the melody's register.
    Spread becomes rhythm
    Tightly clustered colour keeps a sparse arpeggio; widely scattered colour fills it in up to every sixteenth note.
    The video picks its key
    The first strongly coloured frame chooses one of twelve keys and one of three chord progressions, always played in a major pentatonic scale.
